# AEGIS SECURE PRO

Autonomous Multi-Stream Face Recognition Platform

Hybrid AI Architecture: YuNet Detection + ArcFace Embeddings

---

**Aegis Secure Pro** is a four-channel face recognition platform that identifies **real** authorized personnel and non-authorized people in real time across warehouses, laboratories, stores, factories, residential complexes, and data centers. Built on a hybrid AI architecture; combining ultra-fast face detection with state-of-the-art biometric embeddings. It operates entirely offline: no cloud, no internet, no data leaks.

---

## Why Choose Aegis Secure Pro?

**Total Offline and Information Privacy**
No internet, cloud server required. No subscription fees needed. All data stays in local storage. Ideal for environments with strict data-security requirements.

**4 Simultaneous Cameras (Full Environment Coverage)**
Processes up to four camera feeds in parallel: IP cameras, CCTV, commercial cameras (via USB), or pre-recorded video files.
*(NO INFRARED CAMERA NEEDED)*

**New Generation AI and Deep Learning**
YuNet ONNX for ultra-fast face detection, paired with ArcFace (InsightFace) for high-precision biometric matching (Hybrid Architecture). Models trained on millions of faces for highly accurate results.

**Anti-Spoofing (Built-in)**
Optics-based intelligent liveness detection (Laplacian texture and FFT frequency analysis) to neutralize deceptive attempts (printed photos or videos on phone screens). No special hardware needed.

---

## Architectural Highlights

**Instant Enrollment**
Register a new person with a few photos. The system learns their face and adds them to the local database; no cloud round-trip, no waiting.

**Event Logging**
Triggers an immediate alert when a non-allowed presence is detected. Automatically logs the date, time, and camera ID in a secure local SQLite database for follow-up and reporting.

**Real-Time Performance**
GPU-accelerated processing. Live streams stay smooth at 30 FPS while recognition runs across 4 channels in the background.

**Scientific and Practical Rigor (Optical Physics Ph.D.)**
Designed by ***a Ph.D. in Optics & Laser Physics***. Ensuring physical and mathematical precision, algorithmic quality, and expert system support.
